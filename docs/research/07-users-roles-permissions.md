# User Tiers, Roles & Permissions in Support Platforms — Research Report (Aug 2026)

## 1. The incumbent role taxonomies

**Zendesk** ships five staff member types: **Admin, Agent, Light Agent, Contributor, Billing Admin**, plus **End Users** (unlimited, free). Custom roles exist **only on Enterprise plans** (~$169+/agent/mo, now "Suite Enterprise + Copilot," sales-gated), where you can create up to **197 custom agent roles**. Below Enterprise you get fixed roles only. Agent permissions and light-agent group scoping interact in non-obvious ways — Zendesk's own docs warn that misconfigured light agents and contributors can silently **consume paid agent seats** (there's a dedicated help article, "Discovering light agents and contributors that also take up paid agent seats").

**Freshdesk** splits the model along two axes admins must configure separately: **Agent Role** (what actions: Admin, Supervisor, Agent, plus custom roles on Pro+) and **Ticket Scope** (what tickets are visible: Global / Group / Restricted, with an "Advanced ticket scope" adding view-only group access). Freshdesk's docs explicitly teach "Ticket Scope vs. Agent Role — what's the difference?" — evidence the two-axis split confuses users. Agent types: **Full-time**, **Occasional** (billed by **Day Passes**, ~$2–$15/pass depending on plan; being phased out), and **Collaborators**.

## 2. How vendors charge for read-only / collaborator seats (the pain point)

- **Zendesk Light Agents**: can view tickets (group-scoped or all), add **private-only** comments, use email side conversations; cannot reply publicly, be assignees, change status, or @mention. Included counts: **Growth 50, Professional 100, Enterprise 1,000, Enterprise Plus 5,000 — but $0 on Suite Team ($55/agent/mo), where it's a paid add-on**. So the cheapest real plan can't loop in an engineer without paying. Light agent permissions are **barely editable**: Growth/Pro can set ticket scope + reporting view-only; only Enterprise adds Explore options; everything else is locked ("as a system-defined role, admins have no option to edit it" is a recurring G2/community complaint).
- **Freshdesk Collaborators**: up to **5,000 included, but only on Pro ($55) and Enterprise ($89)** — Free and Growth ($19) get none. Two flavors: **Ticket Collaborator** (tagged on tickets, private notes, can update status, no public replies) and **Analytics Collaborator** (adds report viewing). Occasional agents (day passes) are full-capability but per-day billed.
- **Intercom**: **Lite seats free but plan-gated** — 20 free on Advanced ($85/seat/mo), 50 on Expert ($132); Essential ($29) gets none.
- **Help Scout**: after abandoning its 2024 contacts-based experiment, back to per-user pricing (Free / $25 / $45 / $75 per user/mo) with **Light Users**: 5 on Free, 15 Standard, 25 Plus, 50 Pro; **extras $10/user/mo**. Advanced permissions ("control visibility at company/team/user level") are **Pro-only**.
- **HubSpot Service Hub** (seat model since Mar 2024): **View-Only seats free and unlimited**; Core seats $15 (Starter) / ~$90–100 (Pro) / $150 (Enterprise).
- **Jira Service Management**'s long-standing wedge: unlimited free "customers," pay only for agents.
- **B2B-native challengers**: **Pylon** — Starter $59, Professional $89, Enterprise $139/seat/mo with **3-seat minimums (7 on Enterprise)** and add-ons that balloon cost (Account Intelligence $10/account/mo with a 50-account minimum = $500/mo floor). **Plain** — Foundation $35, Horizon $89/seat/mo, AI agent bundled, no per-resolution fees (but added metered "credits" for its Sidekick assistant in July 2026).

**Actionable pattern**: the universal complaint (documented across G2/Trustpilot/Reddit per TrustRadius and pricing analyses) is paying full price for occasional users — "a CEO who logs in once a quarter pays the same as power users." Gating collaborator seats behind mid/upper tiers (Zendesk Team, Freshdesk Growth, Intercom Essential all exclude them) is exactly where churn-driving resentment sits. **Free unlimited light/viewer seats on every tier is a proven differentiator** (HubSpot, JSM) and cheap to offer.

## 3. End-customer portals & B2B account visibility

**Zendesk**: End users see only their own requests by default. Two mechanisms widen this: (a) **Shared organization** — an org-level "Users can view all org tickets" flag, or per-user grant; conflict resolution is "most permissive wins." In shared orgs users can view org-mates' tickets but **can only reply if CC'd first** — a documented friction point. (b) **CCs and Followers**: CCs are visible/external; Followers are internal-only silent subscribers. Users can belong to **multiple organizations (up to 300) — but not on Team plan** (1 org/user), so basic B2B modeling is tier-gated. Community threads ("How can an end-user see all the tickets assigned to an organization," "Can I automatically CC all users in an organization?") show demand for a **customer-side admin role** — someone at the client company who manages their org's tickets/users — which Zendesk still lacks as a first-class concept.

**Freshdesk**: **Contacts + Companies**; a per-contact checkbox "Can see all tickets from this company." Contacts can belong to **multiple companies** with per-company visibility grants. Community threads repeatedly ask for this to be **default-on per company** ("'Can see all tickets from this company' Default" idea thread) and for **CC'd colleagues to see tickets in the portal** — both still gaps. **SLAs per account tier**: multiple SLA policies by company/company-segment (e.g., a "VIP" segment) require **Pro**; custom-field conditions and 5-level conditions require **Enterprise**. Zendesk similarly supports org-based SLA policies; Pylon makes per-account SLAs + account owners core objects.

## 4. Multi-brand / multi-tenant

Zendesk multibrand: **Team 1 brand, Growth/Professional 5 brands, Enterprise/Plus 300**. Restricting **agents** to specific brands is Enterprise-only (custom roles). Each brand gets its own help center + email/portal. Freshdesk handles this via **multiple products/portals** (plan-gated: Pro allows 5 products, Enterprise unlimited). This matters for agencies/MSPs — a segment underserved at low tiers.

## 5. Review complaint themes (G2/Capterra/Reddit/community)

- **Zendesk**: "Ease of Setup" is its **lowest G2 sub-score**; median implementation 4–12 weeks; admin-side complexity rated far worse than agent UX. Specific gripes: uneditable Light Agent role, custom roles locked to Enterprise, role/permission sprawl, per-agent + Copilot ($50/agent/mo add-on) + per-resolution AI stacking.
- **Freshdesk**: confusing settings layout ("not clear where settings are located"), **limited granular permissions**, portal login friction, weak portal customization, and the Role-vs-Scope two-axis confusion.
- **Cross-platform**: paying agent prices for read-only stakeholders; org ticket visibility being all-or-nothing; customer-side self-administration missing.

## 6. What a "simple but sufficient" model looks like (grounded in the gaps)

1. **Four internal roles cover ~95% of needs**: Owner/Admin, Supervisor (reporting + team mgmt), Agent, and **free unlimited Collaborator** (view + private notes + @mention) on **every plan** — directly attacking Zendesk Team's add-on and Freshdesk Growth's zero-collaborator gap.
2. **Separate "what you can do" from "what you can see"** like Freshdesk, but with one UI: role picker + scope picker on the same screen, sane defaults.
3. **First-class Company object with a customer-admin role**: a portal user who can see/manage all company tickets, invite teammates, and set who sees what — the single most-requested missing feature in both vendors' communities. Per-company default visibility toggle (not per-contact).
4. **SLA policies attachable to a company or plan-tier field on every paid plan** — Freshdesk gates this at $55/agent.
5. **Custom roles as an upsell is fine (that's the norm), but never lock the collaborator/viewer tier or basic org visibility behind it.**
6. **Multi-brand: 2–3 brands on the base plan** beats Zendesk Team's 1.
7. KB/SOP tie-in: permission KB articles and internal SOPs by audience (public / logged-in customers / specific companies / internal-only) — Zendesk Guide user-segment gating is Professional+; making it base-tier is another wedge.
