# Open Questions & Research Caveats

*Critical review of the research reports in this directory — what remains unvalidated, which claims conflict, and the five most decision-relevant insights. Read this before treating any single number in the reports as settled.*

## 1. Unanswered questions

- **ICP undefined**: no report says who we sell to first (team size, B2B vs B2C, vertical) or their actual willingness-to-pay — every pricing recommendation is unanchored.
- **Unit economics of "AI included flat"**: zero research on LLM inference cost per resolution; flat pricing could be margin suicide at scale. This underpins the #1 recommendation and is unvalidated.
- **Migration**: no research on how teams actually leave Zendesk/Freshdesk (data export, macros, KB import, switching costs) — churn intent ≠ churn ability.
- **Channel feasibility**: WhatsApp is called decisive, but Meta Business API costs, approval friction, and BSP economics are unresearched.
- **Demand validation for KB+SOP+helpdesk combo**: "white space" may be no-demand space — nobody tested whether buyers want one tool vs. best-of-breed.
- **Compliance table stakes** (SOC 2, GDPR, HIPAA) for winning even SMB deals: absent.
- **Business model**: OSS/self-hosted vs SaaS is never decided; the opensource report implies one, the rest imply the other.
- **Salesforce Service Cloud / ServiceNow** omitted entirely; quantified incumbent churn rates never sourced.

## 2. Suspect / inconsistent claims

- **Zendesk Enterprise price**: cited as "~$115+" (freshdesk report) and "~$169+" (zendesk, roles reports) — reconcile before modeling.
- **Freddy AI contradictions**: plan prices differ across reports ($49/$79 vs $55/$89), and free-session eligibility conflicts ("Pro/Enterprise only" vs "Growth/Pro/Enterprise").
- **Chatwoot pricing conflicts**: trends says "Pro $75/mo unlimited agents"; opensource says per-agent $19/$39/$99 tiers. One is wrong or stale.
- **Help Scout**: $25/user per-seat (competitors) vs "$50/mo with 100 contacts" (trends) — the dual-model story needs verification.
- **Salesforce–Fin $3.6B acquisition**: enormous, unclosed, single-sourced — verify before basing strategy on Intercom uncertainty.
- **Zendesk "80% voice resolution"** is vendor marketing directly contradicted by the 14% industry self-service resolution stat cited elsewhere.
- **Source bias**: heavy reliance on competitor content-marketing blogs (eesel, Richpanel, Macha, Voiceflow) systematically inflates incumbent negatives; "92% would use a KB" is a recycled decade-old vendor stat.
- **OSS cost math** ($280 FreeScout vs $41K Zendesk) ignores admin/ops labor — misleading.

## 3. Five most decision-relevant insights

- **Metered AI billing (per resolution/session, charging for failures) is the most-documented cross-vendor resentment — flat, included AI is the single strongest positioning wedge.**
- **No product anywhere (SaaS or OSS) combines customer KB + verified internal SOPs + ticketing with tiered visibility — but it's unvalidated white space.**
- **Free unlimited collaborator seats + a customer-side org-admin role are cheap, review-proven differentiators every incumbent gates.**
- **KB quality caps AI performance (realistic deflection 38–50%, not 65–80%) — making KB authoring the day-one workflow solves both cold-start and honesty positioning.**
- **Timing: Freshdesk CX growing only 4% (starved of investment), Peppermint archived, UVdesk dormant — two "simple alternative" market slots are open now, and Libredesk (no KB yet) is the fastest rival for them.**
